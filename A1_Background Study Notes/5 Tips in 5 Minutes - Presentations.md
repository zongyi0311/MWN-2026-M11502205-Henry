# Video Notes | 5 Tips in 5 Minutes – Presentations

Video: [5 Tips in 5 Minutes – Presentations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YVgS_opYacQ)  

## Main Idea

Presentation slides should support the speaker rather than serve as a complete script. Effective slides are clear, simple, visually meaningful, and consistent.

## Five Key Tips

1. **Keep it simple**

   Each slide should communicate one main idea. Avoid overcrowding the slide with too much information.

2. **Use fewer words**

   Include only keywords, short phrases, and important numbers. Explain the details verbally instead of putting the entire script on the slide.

3. **Use meaningful visuals**

   Images, icons, and videos should help explain the topic. Visuals should not be added only for decoration.

4. **Keep charts and graphs easy to understand**

   Use simple charts and highlight the most important data or trend. Remove unnecessary details so the audience can understand the main conclusion quickly.

5. **Follow a consistent template**

   Use consistent fonts, colors, layouts, and title positions throughout the presentation. This makes the presentation look more professional and easier to follow.

## My Understanding

This video shows that a presentation is not about displaying every piece of information. Instead, the presenter should select the most important points and guide the audience through them.

When creating future presentations, I will first identify the main message of each slide. Then, I will remove unnecessary text, add useful visuals, and make sure the design is consistent throughout the presentation.

## Presentation Checklist

- Does each slide have only one main idea?
- Can I remove any unnecessary words?
- Do the visuals help explain the content?
- Can the audience understand the chart quickly?
- Are the fonts, colors, and layouts consistent?
- Can I explain the topic without simply reading the slides?
